Community
=========

There is a Google group dedicated to sqlkit: subscribe or browse the archives
at http://groups.google.com/group/sqlkit to ask questions, report bugs or
share feedback.

The sources live in a Mercurial repository at
http://hg.argolinux.org/py/sqlkit/.
