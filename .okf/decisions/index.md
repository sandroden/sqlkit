# Decisions

Durable engineering decisions for sqlkit, grounded on the `changelog` and repo
history.

* [Python 3 / GTK 3 port](py3-gtk3-port.md) - the port from the Py2/GTK2 origin.
* [SQLAlchemy 2.x support](sqlalchemy-2-support.md) - dual 1.4 + 2.x baseline via a compat shim.
* [GTK 4 evaluation](gtk4-evaluation.md) - investigated, not started; in-cell editing is the key risk.
